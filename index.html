How to log RIR

Reps in reserve: how many more clean reps you could have done. Record it for the final working set of each exercise. That's enough to start calibrating intensity.

§ 3 · PROGRESSIVE OVERLOAD

Weight first, reps follow

We prioritize load increases, but not at the expense of productive reps. The objective is progressive mechanical tension with consistent technique, not heavier numbers at any cost.

!

Big jumps on small lifts. Lateral raises from 4 to 5 kg is +25%. If the heavier dumbbells make you swing, shorten the range or shrug, return to 4 kg. We're growing the lateral delts, not just moving heavier dumbbells.

i

What changed from Circuit 1. Circuit 1 used double progression: reps first, load once every set hit the top. Circuit 2 leads with load and lets reps drop back into a lower range.
