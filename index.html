§ 9 · PROGRESSION

Double progression, the most important part

This is how we avoid another six months of identical prescriptions. Reps climb first. Load rises only when the current load is genuinely mastered.

Incline machine press · 3 × 8–12

Five sessions in the life of one exercise
Example

The rule

  • 1Keep the load. Add reps inside the range, session by session.
  • 2When every set reaches the top of the range at the target RIR, increase the weight next session.
  • 3Reps drop after the jump. That is expected. Start rebuilding.
i

Lateral raises use the same concept across a wider rep range.

!

Small increases win. If the cable stack jumps too far, extend the rep range or use small add-on weights before you sacrifice form.

§ 11 · EXECUTION PLAN

The 55-minute session

Where the time goes in a typical session. Circuit 1 sessions are planned at 50–55 minutes. Warm-up sets don't count toward the 85 weekly working sets.

!

Push B and Pull B are the most likely to run past 55 minutes. Time them during the first two weeks.

Pull B has seven exercises and 15 sets; Push B has 15 sets including six of lateral raises. If they consistently run long, remove or move the lowest-value accessory. Don't cut rest so far that set performance suffers.

Walking and cardio

The mandatory daily 5 km walk as a calorie-burning requirement is retired. Walking can stay in your routine and easy cardio is fine. As leg volume rises, recovery matters more than hitting an exercise-calorie target, so cardio should never compromise lower-body training.

NUTRITION · 8-WEEK LADDER

Climbing toward maintenance

Protein stays at 160 g, fat rises slowly and carbs take most of the added calories to fuel training. Weekly numbers are flexible reference points, not ceilings. Higher-calorie meals and weekends are normal life, with no need to compensate afterwards.

Weekly calorie reference

!

1,700 kcal is likely still a deficit at your activity level. Eat above the reference whenever hunger, training or social occasions call for it. We aren't prolonging the cut.

✓

A guide, not a test. 2,100 kcal in week 1 is not a mistake. If recovery clearly needs more food, we don't wait for the next scheduled step.

i

Weeks 5–8 are provisional. They move with weight trend, appetite, energy and performance, and don't climb automatically past maintenance. Calories are approximate due to rounding.

i

Day 7 is no exception. Same weekly reference on recovery days; there's no need to cut calories.

The goal for the first month isn't to maximize weight gain. It's to establish the routine, improve energy availability and drop the psychological burden of cutting.

From there, the winter is for building muscle with a modest surplus.
